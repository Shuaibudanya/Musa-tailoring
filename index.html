About Musa Tailoring

Rooted in Kano, built stitch by stitch

Musa Tailoring has spent years dressing the men and women of Kano in kaftans, jalabiyas and traditional Hausa outfits made the proper way: measured on you, cut by hand, and finished with the kind of stitching that lasts through years of wear.

We work from your measurements or a design you bring us, and we don't hand it back until the fit is right. No two customers get the same shortcuts.

  • Made to your exact measurements
  • Traditional and modern styles
  • Fittings and adjustments included
